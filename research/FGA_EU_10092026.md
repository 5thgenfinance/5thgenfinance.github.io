## Executive Summary

enCore Energy is a Tier 1 (USA) uranium in-situ recovery (ISR) producer with Alta Mesa (70%) and the Rosita plant in South Texas, plus a development pipeline in Texas, South Dakota and Wyoming [1][2]. The Durrett 10-factor score is 6.1 / 10. Our sum of the parts (1.3x AISC, stage factors, 5% discount rate, fully diluted shares) gives $0.74, $1.41 and $3.66 per share at U3O8 of $85, $100 and $150, against a price near $1.08 [3]. Analyst targets were not retrieved, so no base-case comparison is shown.

Main drags: weak H1 2026 extraction (131,274 lb vs 317,613 lb a year earlier; $57.36/lb extraction cost) and a 360,000 lb spot purchase in Q2 to meet deliveries [1][8], a CEO change in April 2026 [8], thin cash against $115M of convertible notes [1][4], a new US$250M at-the-market (ATM) equity program larger than the market cap [14], and Dewey-Burdock permitting risk, about half of project NAV [6]. Recommendation: Hold. Data Quality Score 6.75 / 10. NOTE: this report has not been re-audited since Oct 8; several figures (listed in the Data Quality Audit) are provisional.

## Red Flags and Investor Concerns

- **Red Flag: Management and contract coverage.** The board replaced the CEO on Apr 20, 2026 and returned the founder as Executive Chair; Q2 2026 needed a 360,000 lb spot purchase to meet deliveries. Pending permits point to relief no earlier than 2027 [8].
- **Red Flag: Dilution capacity.** The US$250M ATM is about 1.2x the market cap, and cash of $21.8M is thin against $115M of notes [1][14].
- **Red Flag: Execution.** H1 2026 extraction was 131,274 lb vs 317,613 lb a year earlier; extraction cost rose to $57.36/lb [1].
- **Red Flag: Dewey-Burdock permitting.** About half of project NAV depends on a project still seeking state permits [6].
- **Red Flag: People (5), Share Structure (6), Good Buzz (4), Cash / Debt (4).** Thin liquidity against $115M of notes, weak price action and potential dilution [1][3][4].
