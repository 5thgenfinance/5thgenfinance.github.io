## Executive Summary

Paladin is a uranium producer and developer with a 75% interest in the producing Langer Heinrich Mine (LHM) in Namibia (Tier 2) and 100% of the Patterson Lake South (PLS) project in Saskatchewan, Canada (Tier 1) [1][3]. The Durrett 10-factor score is 6.6 / 10. Our sum of the parts (1.3x AISC, stage factors, 5% discount rate, basic shares) gives $4.04, $6.73 and $15.72 per share at U3O8 of $85, $100 and $150, against a price near $6.09 [5]. The $100 case is well below the Investing.com analyst average of about $9.48 [6].

Main drags: PLS needs about US$1.23B of capital against US$265M of cash, with FID not targeted until end-2027 [1][3][12]; LHM full-cost margins are thin at $85 uranium (June-quarter cost of production US$51.6/lb, excluding stockpile, stripping, royalties, G&A and sustaining capital) [1][2][13]; and the CNSC has not set a PLS hearing date [11]. Recommendation: Hold with downside bias. Data Quality Score 6.35 / 10.

## Red Flags and Investor Concerns

- **Red Flag: PLS funding gap.** US$1.23B of capital vs US$265M of cash; FID not until end-2027; no financing committed [1][3][12].
- **Red Flag: Licensing timeline.** The CNSC found the PLS application sufficient but has not set a hearing date [11][12].
- **Red Flag: Langer Heinrich margins.** Cost of production US$51.6/lb in the June quarter, guided US$44-48/lb for FY2027, excluding stockpile, stripping, royalties, G&A and sustaining capital; a full AISC near $62 leaves little margin at $85 uranium [1][2][13].
- **Red Flag: Partner and jurisdiction.** 25% of Langer Heinrich belongs to a Chinese state-owned partner; Namibia is Tier 2 [1].
- **Red Flag: Good Buzz (4), Upside (5).** Price near $6.09 after a roughly 5% drop on Oct 7; Goldman only moved to Neutral on Oct 8 [5][6].
