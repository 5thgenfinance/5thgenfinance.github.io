## Executive Summary

Uranium Energy Corp (UEC) is a Tier 1 (USA / Canada) uranium producer and developer operating two ISR mines, Christensen Ranch in Wyoming and Burke Hollow in Texas, with no debt and US$495M of cash at Jul 31, 2026 [1]. The Durrett 10-factor score is 6.2 / 10. Our sum of the parts (1.3x AISC, stage factors, 5% discount rate, fully diluted shares) gives $3.99, $5.36 and $9.95 per share at U3O8 of $85, $100 and $150, against a price near $9.47 [5]. The $100 case is well below the analyst average target of about $16.1 [6].

The formula values only compliant resources. It excludes Sweetwater (about 175 Mlb of historical, non-compliant resources and a licensed mill bought for about $175M), the conversion and titanium projects, which explains much of the gap to the market price [2][12]. Other drags are a slow ramp (229,294 lb in FY2026) with no FY2027 guidance [1][9], and heavy equity issuance, including a US$600M ATM program [7]. Recommendation: Hold. Data Quality Score 6.20 / 10.

## Red Flags and Investor Concerns

- **Red Flag: Valuation.** The price is above the Durrett value even at $150 uranium; the market is paying for Sweetwater, Roughrider and conversion plans the formula does not value [2][5][12].
- **Red Flag: Production ramp and regulation.** FY2026 production was 229,294 lb against about 4 Mlb/yr licensed capacity per hub; Christensen Ranch costs swung from $54.61/lb in Q3 to $35.63/lb in Q4; no FY2027 guidance [1][9].
- **Red Flag: Dilution.** US$449M of net equity in six months, an open US$600M ATM and 503.3M fully diluted shares [4][7].
- **Red Flag: Resource quality.** Sweetwater's 175 Mlb is historical and not compliant; Wyoming and Burke Hollow figures are company-presentation numbers; Roughrider has no reserves and 54% inferred pounds [2][3].
