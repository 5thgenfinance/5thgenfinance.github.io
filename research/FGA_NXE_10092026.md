## Executive Summary

NexGen is a Tier 1 (Canada) uranium developer building Rook I, a high-grade underground mine and mill in Saskatchewan's Athabasca Basin. It received its construction licence on Mar 5, 2026 and began a four-year build with a groundbreaking on Aug 13, 2026, pointing to first production around 2030 [5][9]. The Durrett 10-factor score is 6.6 / 10. Our sum of the parts (1.3x AISC, stage factors, 5% discount rate, fully diluted shares) gives $11.17, $13.88 and $22.90 per share at U3O8 of $85, $100 and $150, against a price near $8.99 [3]. The $100 case is close to the simple average analyst target of about $14.50 [8].

Main drags: a single-asset company; pre-production capex of C$2.2B, well above the 2021 feasibility estimate [2]; liquidity plus held uranium of about US$0.96B against about US$1.56B of capex, with project debt not yet closed [1][2]; a 733.9M fully diluted share count [4]; and a price about 35% below its January high [3]. Recommendation: Hold / accumulate on weakness. Data Quality Score 6.60 / 10.

## Red Flags and Investor Concerns

- **Red Flag: Construction and capex.** Pre-production capex is C$2.2B, up from about C$1.3B in the 2021 feasibility study (prov.), with a four-year build [2][9].
- **Red Flag: Funding gap.** Cash plus held uranium covers roughly 60% of the full capex budget; no project debt has closed [1][2].
- **Red Flag: Single asset and licensing.** All value sits in Rook I; the construction licence does not authorize operation [5].
- **Red Flag: Share Structure (4), Low Valuation (4), Good Buzz (5), Upside (5).** 733.9M diluted shares, about $22 EV per plausible lb, a price about 35% below the January close high, SOTP at $150 of 2.5x the price [3][4].
