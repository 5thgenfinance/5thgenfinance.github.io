Plausible = 0.9 × P&P + 0.65 × (M&I inclusive minus reserves) + 0.3 × inferred.

Tumas: 79.5 × 0.9 + (102.1 − 79.5) × 0.65 + 16.1 × 0.3 = 91.070 Mlb; ×95% = 86.5165 Mlb.

Mulga Rock: 42.3 × 0.9 + (64.4 − 42.3) × 0.65 + 40.4 × 0.3 = 64.555 Mlb.
