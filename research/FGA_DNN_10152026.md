### Operating Value

Annual pounds = attributable plausible pounds / mine life. Cash flow = annual pounds × (scenario price − 1.30×AISC). Complete-year discount timing = start delay + year index + 0.5. Operating NAV = stage × sum of discounted cash flows.

Gryphon's 0.5-year tail and Midwest's 0.14-year tail have proportional output and their own midpoint timing. No extra recovery or permitting probability is applied.
