## Executive Summary

enCore Energy is a U.S. uranium in-situ recovery (ISR) operator with a development pipeline in Texas, South Dakota and Wyoming. The modeled portfolio includes Alta Mesa, Mesteña Grande, Dewey-Burdock and the ISR portion of Gas Hills. The analysis uses 70% economic ownership for Alta Mesa and Mesteña Grande and 100% for Dewey-Burdock and Gas Hills. [S1][S2][S3][S7]

The sum-of-the-parts illustration gives **$1.05, $1.73 and $4.00 per share** at flat U3O8 prices of $85, $100 and $150 per pound. The model applies a 0.83 M&I weight where no proven and probable reserve is reported, a 0.30 inferred weight, a 1.3× cost pad, 5% mid-year discounting, stage-weighted capital and 207.257939M gross diluted shares. [M1]

These are provisional values for a modeled asset subset, not verified whole-company fair values or price targets. Remaining capital, cost components, current balances, retained investments, some liabilities and post-August share movements are incompletely reconciled. Both excluded positive assets and excluded obligations may be material; the illustration is not a reliable valuation floor.

## Valuation Method and Assumptions

For each project, operating NAV is the stage factor multiplied by the sum of discounted annual margins. Annual margin is attributable plausible pounds divided by production years, multiplied by uranium price less 1.30 times the stated cost input. Cash-flow times are start delay plus production-year number minus 0.5, discounted at 5%.

Enterprise value = modeled operating NAV − stage-weighted attributable capital PV.

Equity value = enterprise value + included cash + included securities + marked inventory − note principal.

Value per share = equity value / 207.257939M.

### Cost basis and remaining-capital limitations

- Alta Mesa: the company release gives operating cost of $27.44/lb, ad valorem tax of $0.30 and royalty of $2.61, totaling $30.35 before income tax and separately treated capital. The model uses a $36.42 cost proxy, not a newly verified current AISC. The $25.9M capital budget comprises $2.5M plant refurbishment and $23.4M wellfield development; spent versus remaining costs and inclusion in the cost proxy are unresolved. [S1]
- Mesteña: $25.49 operating + $0.30 ad valorem + $3.60 royalty = $29.39/lb. This operating subtotal is not automatically comprehensive AISC. The $108.1M capital budget includes $94.4M wellfield development and $13.7M facilities. Treating the full budget as remaining pre-production spending is a model simplification, not a verified schedule. Economic ownership of 70% applies to pounds and capital. [S1][S2]
- Gas Hills: the $40.61/lb pre-income-tax headline includes capital and cannot simply replace $30.93 while the same capital is deducted separately. The model's cost-component bridge remains unresolved. Published initial capital is $55.2M. [S1][S4]
- Dewey-Burdock: the capital assumption uses $105M of early spending within a $264.2M total budget. The allocation of later wellfield spending to the $35.01/lb cost proxy remains unresolved; the operating-cost and capital treatment is not independently certified. [S18]
- Juniper Ridge: no NAV is assigned without sufficient cost and capital support.

The 1.3× pad includes a tax provision but is not a statutory tax calculation. Confidence weights, including 0.83, are not physical recoveries. Costs on produced pounds and resources on contained pounds require a separate recovery/mine-plan reconciliation. This simplified model is not equivalent to a technical-study NPV.

## Operating Results and Red Flags

> **Red Flag — Execution:** H1 extraction was 131,274 lb versus 317,613 lb, with $57.36/lb extraction costs. Delivered uranium cost was $75.54/lb against $70.10/lb realized sales, including purchased uranium in delivered costs. These measures differ from modeled AISC and are not interchangeable. [S12]

> **Red Flag — Management:** Richard Little became CEO and William Sheriff returned as Executive Chair on April 20, 2026. Operating performance remains a key assessment criterion. No misconduct or accounting deficiency is inferred from the management changes. [S15]

> **Red Flag — Liquidity and dilution:** The $250M ATM is equity-sale capacity, not cash received, committed credit or shares already issued. June cash is dated; subsequent usage, issuance and cash burn remain unresolved. [S12][S16]

> **Red Flag — Permitting:** BLM authorization and NRC renewal represent material progress at Dewey-Burdock. Remaining state conditions, financing and construction readiness still require official verification; these developments do not establish that every approval is complete. [S13][S14]

> **Red Flag — Cost and capital:** Wellfield spending may be misallocated between operating-cost proxies and separate capital. Remaining-spend schedules and cost components are not fully reconciled. Duplicate deductions would understate value; omitted costs would overstate it.

> **Red Flag — Valuation:** The $100 modeled subset is $1.73 with weighted capital and $1.22 with full retained capital. Excluded assets and liabilities pull in opposite directions. The 0.83 resource factor increases value but does not establish better geology, recovery or economic feasibility.

> **Red Flag — Scorecard limitations:** Sub-6 reference scores are Properties 4, People 5, Buzz 4 and Cash/Debt 4. Share Structure 6 is not below 6. The reference assessment has not been independently revalidated against all current model inputs.
