# IsoEnergy Ltd. (NYSE American: ISOU | TSX: ISO) - Company Review

**Fifth Generation Advisers** | Prepared by Robert Maxwell | Analysis date: October 21, 2026 | Method: Don Durrett 10-factor + sum of the parts (FGA Methodology effective Oct 13, 2026) | All figures USD (CAD at 0.71 USD/CAD)

## Executive Summary

IsoEnergy is a Tier 1 (Canada, Australia, USA) uranium developer with no production and no revenue: Hurricane in Saskatchewan (100%, 48.6 Mlb indicated at 34.5% U3O8), Wiluna in Western Australia (100%, JORC 69.1 Mlb M&I), a 33% stake in DISA Uranium (the Utah mines, including Tony M), and legacy Coles Hill, Matoush and Ben Lomond carried on historical estimates [1][3][5]. The Durrett 10-factor score is 7.0 / 10. Our sum of the parts (1.3x AISC, stage factors, 5% discount rate) gives **$15.46 / $21.38 / $41.10** per fully diluted share at U3O8 of $85 / $100 / $150, against a price near $8.24 [4]. The core assets alone give $13.29 / $17.73 / $32.52; the analyst average target of about C$23 (US$16.60) sits close to that core case [7].

Main drags: costs are assumed for four of five properties and Hurricane capex is a US$250M placeholder; the DISA stake is valued at a private-placement price; legacy estimates are historical; and development capital implies dilution. Recommendation: Accumulate (speculative). Data Quality Score 5.5 / 10; Table 8 labels what remains provisional.

## Capital Raise Outlook (18 months)

Probability of a meaningful raise: about 50%; forced raise: about 5%. Cash of about US$50M plus US$37M securities compares with a burn of about C$30M a year (H1 2026 cash exploration C$10.8M; trailing levered free cash flow about -C$30M) [1][4], Wiluna capex of US$194M and an unquantified Hurricane build. Tony M is funded inside DISA, so restart cash flow accrues to DISA and does not directly reduce IsoEnergy's needs; IsoEnergy may face cash calls to keep its 33%. Analyst judgment, not a sourced forecast.
