## Executive Summary
Ur-Energy is a Wyoming-jurisdiction (Tier 1) uranium in-situ recovery producer in ramp-up, with Lost Creek and Shirley Basin both producing and no reported reserves [1][5]. Output is about 0.45 Mlb/yr against 2.2 Mlb/yr of constructed capacity [1][8]. The Durrett 10-factor score is **5.6 / 10**. Our sum of the parts ($40/lb all-in cost with no 1.3x pad per client instruction, 0.83 weight on M&I, 0.30 on inferred, stage 1.0, 5% discount rate, the contract book as a liability, plus liabilities for future capex and the operating funding gap) gives $1.17, $1.59 and $3.02 per share at U3O8 of $85, $100 and $150, against a price near $0.61 [3]. The $100 base case sits below the analyst average target of $1.80 (three analysts) [4].

The main drags are: (1) a funding gap, with $50.2M of cash against about $87M of capex and operating burn through 2027, and a $120M convertible due 2031 [2]; (2) 5.945 Mlb of undelivered contracts, 77% fixed-priced at about $67-73/lb through 2033 [7]; and (3) a cost input of $40/lb that excludes about $15/lb of development and G&A, against management commentary of $50-55/lb [8]. Recommendation: **Hold**. Data Quality Score 6.0 / 10; audit details are available on request.

## Valuation: Sum of the Parts
Margin per lb = U3O8 price minus the $40 all-in cost, with no 1.3x pad (client instruction): $45 at $85, $60 at $100 and $110 at $150. Attributable plausible pounds (20.5 Mlb) are produced at 0.9, 1.4 and 1.8 Mlb in 2027-2029 and 2.0 Mlb/yr thereafter until exhausted in 2038, discounted at 5% with mid-year timing and stage factor 1.00 (producing, ramp-up). The 5.945 Mlb of undelivered contracts is a separate liability: 77% x contract pounds x (U3O8 price minus the fixed price of $67, rising 3% a year), discounted; the 23% market-linked share is assumed to earn the market price. Future capex ($34M through 2027) and a funding-gap operating burn ($52.7M, $51.7M present value) are deducted.

## Red Flags and Investor Concerns
- **Red Flag: Cost input and AISC definition.** $40/lb is the Q3 cash cost on about 10,000 produced pounds sold; it excludes development ($52.5M in nine months), G&A and non-cash costs, and no pad is applied. Management commentary cited $50-55/lb [2][8]. At $55 the base case falls to $1.01; at $72.50, $0.33.
- **Red Flag: Technology / execution risk.** Q3 Lost Creek drumming fell 25.8% q/q; Shirley Basin shipped 9,379 lb in Q3; 2026 deliveries were cut to 700,000 lb at about $67/lb and 600,000 lb was deferred [1][8]. Evidence for: pounds captured up 73% y/y in Q3. Evidence against: wastewater capacity is not due until Q2 2027.
- **Red Flag: Contract book.** 5.945 Mlb undelivered, 77% fixed-priced through 2033; the modeled spread liability is $52M to $321M depending on price [7].
- **Red Flag: Cash / Debt (3).** $50.2M of cash against a $120M convertible, $52.2M ARO and a $63.3M nine-month operating burn [2].
- **Red Flag: Good Buzz / Chart (3).** Shares fell about 17% on Nov 10 to near the 52-week low of $0.60 [3].
- **Red Flag: Cost / Financing (4).** Expensed development and G&A of about $82M/yr annualized; refinancing the convertible by January 2031 [2].
- **Red Flag: Share Structure (5).** Warrant exercises added 19.1M shares in 2026; convertible conversion would add about 69M [2].

### Observations and Next Steps
Watch for: wastewater plant completion (Q2 2027), Mine Unit 5 approval (Q1 2027), Lost Soldier technical report (year-end 2026), new term contracts near $97/lb, and Q4 2026 results. Verify: convertible face and conversion terms, options and RSUs, contract floors and ceilings, Q4 2026 capex, the Shirley Basin resource basis and the all-in cost definition.
