## Executive Summary

Deep Yellow is a pre-production uranium developer with Tumas (Namibia) and Mulga Rock (Western Australia). Tumas has no final investment decision (FID); the company targets Q4 2026, and first production would be roughly 2029 if that occurs. The provisional score is 6.4/10 (carried forward, not refreshed) and the general opinion is Hold (speculative).

Under the 10/13/2026 methodology, remaining initial capital is multiplied by the project stage factor. Illustrative SOTP values are US$0.934 / US$1.516 / US$3.458 per share at uranium of $85 / $100 / $150. With 100% of remaining capital deducted (required sensitivity, equal to the 10/9 report) they are US$0.674 / US$1.256 / US$3.198.[^1] These are hypothetical outputs, not verified valuations or price targets.

## Tumas Production Timeline and Catalysts

Tumas has no FID, so no production date is firm. Deep Yellow's board deferred plant construction in April 2025 until uranium prices improve. The company still targets an FID in Q4 2026, subject to market conditions. October 9, 2026 releases added a long-term NamWater water supply agreement and local-ownership (Oponona) agreements. A Canaccord/FNArena note (October 2026) describes a Q4 2026 FID as difficult. Engineering was 79% complete at fiscal year-end 2026; bulk earthworks are complete and civil/concrete contracts (about $34M) were awarded in September. The power supply agreement remains conditional on FID.

The company schedule gives wet commissioning within 24 months after FID, with first drums about 2 months after processing starts. An FID in Q4 2026 would imply first drums around 2029. Earlier guidance of early, mid- or 3Q 2027 production assumed an earlier FID and is stale. The model's 3.0-year start (about late 2029) is consistent with this but remains an assumption, not company guidance.

## Valuation: Sum of the Parts

Enterprise value (SOTP) = operating NAVs minus stage-weighted PV of remaining initial capital. Equity = EV + cash. Per share uses 981.442249M.

## Red Flags and Investor Concerns

- **Red Flag: No Tumas FID.** Production is unscheduled; the Q4 2026 FID target depends on uranium prices and funding. Each year of delay lowers value by about $0.05/share in the model.
- **Red Flag: Capital assumption.** US$120.75M is hypothetical; engineering completion is not capital paid.
- **Red Flag: Stage-weighted capital.** Raises value by about $0.26/share versus full capital; not a probability.
- **Red Flag: Share Structure (4).** Complete diluted securities reconciliation.
- **Red Flag: Buzz / Chart (2).** Carried-forward score, not a refreshed market assessment.
- **Red Flag: Funding and partner obligations.** Resolve Oponona, cost-to-complete, overhead, leases and reclamation.
- **Red Flag: Incomplete asset coverage.** Omahola and Alligator River unmodeled; Mulga Rock classification and costs unverified.
